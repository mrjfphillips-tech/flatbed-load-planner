PTV Discovery Coach

AI-Powered Sales Discovery Assistant

Demo Script v2 - Internal Use Only - PTV Logistics - April 2026

Demo Walkthrough ~17 minutes total

Open demo-v2.html in your browser. No login, no server, works offline.

Scene 1 - The Problem 2 min

Say
"Every sales rep has been in a discovery call where they walked out thinking they covered everything - and then realized they never found out who controls the budget. MEDDIC fixes that, but most reps don't use it because it feels like a checklist."
Say
"PTV Discovery Coach solves that. It's a private AI coach that runs during the call. The customer never sees it. The rep has a natural conversation and the app tracks coverage, suggests questions, summarizes each answer in real time, and builds the report when the call ends."

Scene 2 - Business Card Scan 2 min NEW

Action
Click "+ New Account". Click "Scan Business Card". Watch the Matrix-style animation.
Say
"The OCR reads the card and auto-fills every field. It analyzes the title and auto-detects the persona tier and MEDDIC deal role. Select an industry segment and click Create and Start Session."

Scene 3 - GDPR Consent Gate 1 min NEW

Action
GDPR consent screen appears. Point out the Start Session button is hidden - user MUST go through consent.
Say
"Before any recording starts, the app requires explicit consent. This is a hard gate. The buttons start red and turn green when clicked - clear visual feedback that consent has been logged."
Action
Click the consent button. Watch it turn green. Session starts automatically.

Scene 4 - Live Coaching and AI Answer Summaries 3 min NEW

Action
Point to the suggested question, MEDDIC badge, intent score, and "Why ask this" coaching note.
Say
"The app suggests the next question targeting a specific MEDDIC dimension. The intent score tells the rep whether the customer's answer actually covered what the question was trying to uncover."
Action
Click "Accept". The AI Answer Summary panel appears.
NEW FEATURE
This is the AI Answer Summary - the game-changer. After each question, the AI summarizes what the customer said before moving on.
Say
"After the customer answers, the AI pauses and shows a summary of what was captured. The rep reviews it in real time. If the AI missed something, the rep knows immediately. You also see the score delta: '+12 Goals'. Click Continue to Next Question and the cycle repeats."
Action
Click "Continue to Next Question". Accept 2-3 more to show the cycle. Point to MEDDIC meters updating.

Scene 5 - Session End and KPI Scorecard 2 min NEW

Action
Click "End". Session complete screen appears with MEDDIC scorecard.
Say
"The rep gets a full MEDDIC scorecard - every dimension with its score, progress bar, and delta. First call shows 'baseline'. Follow-up calls show green +12, +8 for improvements and red -3 for regression. The app flags weak areas for the next call."
Action
Scroll to the glowing "What's Next?" panel. Click "Build ROI Calculator".
Say
"The What's Next panel gives four clear next steps. The rep always knows the next move. Each button navigates directly to that section."

Scene 6 - ROI Calculator 2 min

Action
Type values: 150 vehicles, 125 miles, $1.50/mile. Watch results calculate.
Say
"Seven value streams, not just mileage. Fields start yellow (estimate) and turn green when confirmed by the customer. The AI auto-populates as the customer mentions numbers."

Scene 7 - Account Overview and Deal Map 1.5 min

Action
Click "Back to Account History", select Acme Logistics. Show the Deal Map and session history with KPI deltas.
Say
"The Deal Map shows stakeholder coverage and multi-threading score. Session cards show MEDDIC deltas per call - green for gains, red for regression. This is how managers track deal health without sitting in on every call."

Scene 8 - Leexi Audio Sync 1.5 min NEW

Action
On the Account Overview, point to the "Leexi Audio Sync" card with the purple Pull and green Push buttons.
Say
"Leexi integration lives right in the account. Two buttons, two directions. Pull brings call transcripts from Leexi for MEDDIC analysis. Push sends session recordings to Leexi for AI transcription and archival."
Action
Click "Pull from Leexi". Show the call list. Click Import on a call. Then close and click "Push to Leexi". Click "Push All Pending".
Say
"Every call ends up in both systems - no data silos. Reps who already use Leexi don't change their workflow. They record in Leexi, we pull the transcript. Reps using our built-in recording can push to Leexi for archival."

Scene 9 - Navigation 30 sec

Action
Click between sections. Point to "Back to Account History" on each page.

Scene 10 - Admin: Question Bank 1 min

Action
Click Admin. Show segment filter and question bank with coaching notes.
Say
"200+ questions across 12 MEDDIC dimensions with industry-specific sets. CSV upload. Each question has a coaching note explaining why it matters."

Closing

Say
"What you've seen is the working prototype with all core workflows functional. The AI answer summaries, GDPR consent gate, session scorecards, What's Next guidance, and bidirectional Leexi sync are all new. The full app with live transcription and CRM integration is in active development."

Key Differentiators

  • AI answer summaries after every question
  • GDPR consent gate - recording blocked until confirmed
  • Session-end KPI scorecard with improvement tracking
  • "What's Next" action panel
  • Matrix-style business card scanner with auto persona detection
  • Bidirectional Leexi sync - Pull transcripts in, Push recordings out
  • Deal map with multi-threading score
  • Industry-specific question tailoring
